I’m near the end of a cycle; And lately I’ve been thinking about what’s coming next — which still is vague.

Three years have passed and I’ve grown. A lot;
But still, I don't feel neither understood nor fully part of something; And I don’t think I’ll ever be.

However, I’ve been questioning everything, because it feels so weird to still want to be anything else in life, when we are facing an apocalyptic landscape which all the crisis are happening simultaneously. When everything seems to be falling apart, I just started to CARE LESS about my actions repercussions;
Which is good for someone who overthinks and also it’s so relieving to know that nobody really cares about what we’re doing or if we’re good enough. I guess that’s one benefit of living in a nihilist era in the middle of a crisis: you start taking risks, as a stress response to the status-quo; And then some surprises may come along the way.

Everything feels suspended; That’s life at the cliff of an orgasm that never comes. I stopped making big plans for the future and start living with the doubt. There’s a big desire to be independent, be seen and valued, as many of my friends also do. But at the same time, it hurts a lot when nothing hits my mail box. Or even when nothing seems to feel right at the moment.

I’ve been swinging between wanting stability and also wanting chaos in my life; In order to find something new, I truly believe that I can’t set myself too comfortably. There’s something interesting in inhabiting this tension; A sense of mystery and danger seems to unravel & Life feels interesting again; I never felt so complete in being attached and detached to everything.

My purest desire is not having to prove nothing to anybody. Not needing someone to validate my worth. Not needing an important job or role in life to validate what I have to say. Not depending on external things to just simply exist. And after all the noise, I wish to disappear.

The world is ending, however I still CARE about something even if it means to fight for a good end.

Love = Effort = Empathy = Listening x Caring x Taking action = Transformation

Some lessons that I’ve learned in life and also in my practice as a communication designer:
YES, it’s important to put effort into things

Putting the constant effort in things
Open your heart for curiosity over fear
Redirecting energy from the ego to the collective:
I’m not creating as an individual, but as part of something even bigger. It makes a lot of sense if creative work is shared.
Being out there for each other; a small act of support can empower a creative.

At the end, I must confess that the communication design degree made me smarter and hotter. What’s next?
